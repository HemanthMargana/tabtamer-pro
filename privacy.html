Privacy Policy for TabTamer Pro

Effective Date: November 2026

TabTamer Pro is built with a privacy-first, local-first architecture. We do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your browsing history, open tabs, or summarized page content to any external server.

1. Data Processed Locally

  • Tab Metadata & Saved Sessions: Stored exclusively inside your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • Page Digest & Visual Filters: Processed in real time inside your browser memory; no webpage text is ever uploaded.
  • Ad & Tracker Blocking: Executed natively by Chrome's declarativeNetRequest engine using local static rules.

2. Payment & License Verification

When you upgrade to TabTamer Pro, payment processing is handled securely by Lemon Squeezy. The extension only transmits your entered license key to validate activation status.

3. Contact

For support or privacy inquiries, contact: support@yourdomain.com

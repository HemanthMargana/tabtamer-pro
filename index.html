Privacy Policy — TabTamer Pro

Effective Date: November 2026  |  Publisher: HK Digital

TabTamer Pro is built with a strict local-first architecture. We do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your browsing history, open tab URLs, or summarized webpage content to any external server.

1. Local-Only Data Processing

  • Tabs & Saved Sessions: All tab metadata and Session Vault entries are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • Page Digest & Visual Filters: Article extraction, dark mode, night tint, and reader view run entirely inside your browser's local memory. No page text is ever uploaded.
  • Ad & Tracker Blocking: Handled locally by the browser's built-in declarativeNetRequest API using static rules.

2. Payments & License Activation

Pro license purchases are processed securely by Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record). When you enter a license key inside the extension, only the license key string and browser platform name are sent to Lemon Squeezy's license validation API (api.lemonsqueezy.com) to verify activation status and device limits.

3. Contact & Support

For billing help, license resets, or privacy questions, contact HK Digital via your Lemon Squeezy order receipt or at support@hkdigital.example.com.

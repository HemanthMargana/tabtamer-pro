Privacy Policy — TabTamer Pro

Effective Date: November 2026  |  Publisher: HK Digital

TabTamer Pro is built with a strict local-first architecture. We do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your browsing history, open tab URLs, or summarized webpage content to any external server.

1. Local-Only Data Processing

  • Tabs & Saved Sessions: All tab metadata and Session Vault entries are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • Page Digest & Visual Filters: Article extraction, dark mode, night tint, and reader view run entirely inside your browser's local memory. No page text is ever uploaded.
  • Network Rules: Handled locally by the browser's built-in declarativeNetRequest API using static bundled rules.
